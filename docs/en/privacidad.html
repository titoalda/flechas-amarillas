Privacy policy

This website only collects data when you reserve a copy of, or ask for information about, “Behind the Yellow Arrows”. It uses no cookies and no third-party trackers.

What data and why

Your name, your email, the number of copies, the shop name and phone if you are a shop and, if you write them, your town and your message. They are used only to handle your reservation or request and to write to you about the book: price, payment and shipping options and, for shops, terms.

Who handles them

Aldara Álvarez González, the author of the book. For any question, reply to the email we send you or write it in the form.

Legal basis and how long we keep them

Your consent, given when you tick the box in the form. We keep the data while the reservation is open and delete it once the book is delivered or the reservation is cancelled, or earlier if you ask.

Who we share them with

With two services acting as data processors: the one that sends the form by email (Web3Forms), which only passes your message on to the author, and the nanuve.net statistics server, which receives the visit notices described above. They are not shared with anyone else.

Cookies and analytics

This website uses no cookies. To know how many people visit it and from where, it counts visits with its own system, with no cookies and no tracking across sites. For each visit it records the page, where the visitor comes from, the device type, browser, operating system, language, screen width, country and IP address. The IP address is kept for one year and then deleted; it is not used to identify you and is not shared with anyone. These notices are received by a nanuve.net server, with no intermediaries, and the country is worked out there from a public database, without sending your IP to third parties. The legal basis is the legitimate interest in knowing how the website is used (art. 6.1.f GDPR). The fonts are served from the site itself.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent, at any time. If you think they have not been handled properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).
