Privacy policy

This website only collects data when you reserve a copy of, or ask for information about, “Behind the Yellow Arrows”. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party trackers.

What data and why

Your name, your email, the number of copies, the shop name and phone if you are a shop and, if you write them, your town and your message. They are used only to handle your reservation or request and to write to you about the book: price, payment and shipping options and, for shops, terms.

Who handles them

Aldara Álvarez González, the author of the book. For any question, reply to the email we send you or write it in the form.

Legal basis and how long we keep them

Your consent, given when you tick the box in the form. We keep the data while the reservation is open and delete it once the book is delivered or the reservation is cancelled, or earlier if you ask.

Who we share them with

Nobody. They are stored on our own server, and each reservation is notified by email.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent, at any time. If you think they have not been handled properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).
